EverlookDB Pets: hunter pets for WoW Forever.

### Your pet
Name, family, level next to yours, loyalty, free training points, happiness and what it eats. It tells you when your pet falls behind your level.

### Abilities
Every ability your pet's family can learn, with the rank it has and what to do next:

- Train now: you know a higher rank than your pet has.
- Buy the next rank from a pet trainer.
- Learn it from another of your pets (active or stabled).
- Tame a beast that knows it, with a "Where?" button: the best beasts for that rank, the ones you can tame at your level first, nearest first.
- Abilities new in WoW Forever (Dismember, Pinch, Swipe...) are included.

### Still to learn
What your pet can still learn, most worth it first, with the training point cost of every rank.

### Bestiary
Every tameable beast as a card: family, levels, rare or elite, zones, attack speed and every rank it teaches. Search "bite 6", "cat", "stranglethorn", "rare", "fast" or a level. Filter by family, zone, near your level, rare, untamed and your wishlist.

### A beast's page
Its 3D model, levels, attack speed, diet, where it spawns with waypoints (TomTom when loaded, else the game's map pin), and each rank it teaches.

/epets opens it. Data from EverlookDB.com.
